Summary

One accountable path from first idea to post-launch support.

This service combines strategic planning, website or landing page creation, campaign readiness, technical implementation, measurement setup, and maintenance. It is built for teams that do not want strategy, design, development, and support to operate as separate disconnected workstreams.

Problems this service solves

Disconnected vendors

Strategy, design, development, and launch support often move in different directions when ownership is split across too many hands.

Unclear delivery path

Teams know they need a better website or campaign system, but do not yet have a practical sequence of work.

Weak post-launch rhythm

After release, many sites are left without reporting, maintenance, iteration, or a useful improvement backlog.

Launch risk

Forms, tracking, page speed, content, redirects, and QA need to be checked before a public launch or campaign push.

What is included

  • Discovery session covering business goals, target audience, offer structure, current assets, competitors, and constraints.
  • Strategy and delivery roadmap with priorities, page requirements, campaign readiness tasks, and launch milestones.
  • Website, service page, or landing page architecture with clear content blocks and conversion paths.
  • Web design direction prepared for responsive implementation and future visual refinement.
  • Frontend and backend development for pages, forms, validation, simple integrations, and maintainable code structure.
  • Analytics, reporting recommendations, QA checks, launch support, and post-launch maintenance planning.

FAQ

Is this a fixed package?

No. The full-cycle service is scoped around the project. The monthly ranges on the services page are planning references, and the concrete cost can be confirmed with a manager after a short brief.

Can we start with only one part?

Yes. Some clients start with strategy or web design first, then expand into development, launch support, or monthly maintenance.

Does it include ongoing support?

It can. Maintenance, reporting, improvements, content updates, and campaign landing page iterations can be included as a monthly support scope.
