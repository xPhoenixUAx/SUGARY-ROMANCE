FAQs

Have questions in your mind?
Get the answers now.

What services does Sugary Romance Media offer?

We support marketing strategy, paid advertising, web design, website development, landing pages, frontend and backend development, analytics, launch support, and ongoing maintenance.

How do you approach a new project?

We start with business goals, audience, current assets, service needs, budget range, timeline, and launch constraints. From there, we recommend the most practical project path.

What industries do you specialize in?

The process works best for service businesses, B2B companies, growing brands, and teams preparing serious websites, campaigns, or digital systems.

Do you work with startups or established companies?

Both. The main requirement is a clear business need and willingness to shape the website or campaign around practical goals, not just surface-level visuals.

Do you offer custom website development?

Yes. We can build custom frontend layouts, backend form handling, integrations, landing pages, and maintainable website structures without relying on a fixed template.

What is your pricing model or cost structure?

Pricing depends on scope, timeline, technical complexity, number of pages, content needs, integrations, and post-launch support. The contact form includes a budget range so we can respond realistically.

Can you help with ongoing support?

Yes. Support can include content updates, technical fixes, analytics review, campaign landing page improvements, maintenance, and new feature planning.

How do we start?

Send a project inquiry with your goals, preferred service, timeline, budget range, and current website context. We will reply by email with the next useful step.
